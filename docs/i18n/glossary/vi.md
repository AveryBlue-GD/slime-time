# Vietnamese (vi) glossary

Standard Vietnamese, light and playful game register. The player is addressed as `BẠN` only where a
sentence needs a subject (`BẠN ĐÃ THOÁT!`); buttons and hints use bare imperatives (`CHỌN MÀU`,
`NHẤN ĐỂ MUA`). Everything in capitals with all tone marks (`Ấ Ầ Ẩ Ẫ Ậ Ơ Ư`), NFC (one code point per
letter). Western digits, `.` decimals. Straight quotes and `-` as in the English; `!` / `?` without a
space. Vietnamese words are longer than English ones: widths were measured with the slimy font
(`pixel.fnt` xadvance) against every `budget: max Npx` and against the real button / tile sizes.

## Names

- Levels: MỎ RÊU, ĐIỆN ĐÁ CÁT, ĐỀN CHÌM, ĐƯỜNG HẦM ĐỘC, HẦM MỘ RÙNG RỢN, PHÁO ĐÀI BĂNG, NGỤC ĐÁ ĐEN,
  HANG PHA LÊ, HẦM NÚI LỬA, NGỤC TỐI TĂM (alliteration kept where it came naturally: MỎ RÊU's M/R,

- Skins: CHANH (lime), BẠC HÀ, CHANH VÀNG (lemon), ĐẠI DƯƠNG, NHO, ĐỎ THẪM, BĂNG GIÁ, KẸO CAO SU,

## Doubts for a native reviewer

- `SLIME` / `SKIN` / `SEED` kept as loanwords instead of `CHẤT NHỜN` / `TRANG PHỤC` / `HẠT GIỐNG`:
  natural for gamers, but check that it reads well to younger players.
- Tagline `THOÁT KHỎI SƯƠNG MÙ` / `HAY LÀ CHẾT KHÔ`: "die dry" replaces the "dry trying" pun; `chết khô`
  is a real expression but maybe a touch dark for kids. Alternative: `HOẶC KHÔ HÉO`.
- `CHÉN THÁNH` for the chalice (Holy Grail sense) vs. the plainer `CHÉN VÀNG`.
- `LẶN SÂU` as the Descent mode name, `HŨ` for the pot, `BÙA` for boons, `CỔNG NGẪU NHIÊN` for the
  randomizer: invented game terms, check they feel right.
- `GOO NOSE` -> `MŨI NHỚT` (no pun kept); `SLIMAKAZE` kept as is (kamikaze pun only works via English).
- `BẸP.` for the opening `SPLAT.` of story line 1 (onomatopoeia).
- Story line 8 `BẠN THÌ KHÓ MÀ QUÊN` (unforgettable), line 7 `NÓ RUNG RINH` (jiggles): check the deadpan.
- `ANY COLOR YOU LIKE` -> `MÀU NÀO TÙY THÍCH`: the Henry Ford joke is not known in Vietnamese; kept
  the literal meaning.
- `REPLAY MAZE` and `REPLAY` both `CHƠI LẠI` (never shown together; RESTART also `CHƠI LẠI`).
- Tight spots: `HỖ TRỢ +{percent}%` HUD pod is 57 px with 2 digits (budget 60, English is the same);
  `TIẾP TỤC` on the 56 px RESUME button; heat modifier names in 84 px toggles.
- `MẤT LƯỚT MIỄN PHÍ` (brittle: "no free slide"): unsure what "free slide" refers to in game.
- Fix pass F4: `skins.wearing` is (ĐANG DÙNG), not (ĐANG ĐỘI, "on the head"): the key is shared by hats and trails.
