# Glossary: Simplified Chinese (zh_CN, mainland)

Register: plain, informal, no 您; buttons are 2-4 character verbs or nouns. No spaces between words;
full-width `，。！？：（）、` in text, `{placeholders}`, digits and Latin units half-width. Layout gaps from
the English (`  `, `   `) are kept. Widths measured with Fusion Pixel 10 px zh_hans (Hanzi and
full-width punctuation 10 px each) plus the slimy font for digits / Latin (6 px), placeholders filled
with 888: every entry is inside its `max Npx` budget.

## Doubts for a native reviewer

1. **SLIMAKAZE = 史莱玛卡兹** (sound only, pun lost). 神风 (kamikaze) was avoided on purpose: WWII connotations in
   mainland China. Alternative with a pun: 史莱敢死 (史莱姆 + 敢死队, "slime daredevil").
2. **CHAMELEON = 卡魅龙**: the style guide asks for a sound transliteration; a plain 变色龙 may read better.
3. **INKKY = 印奇**: alternative 因奇.
4. **Tagline** `ESCAPE THE FOG / OR DRY TRYING` = 逃离迷雾 / 不干不休: pun on 不死不休 ("never stop till dead");
   in simplified 干 is both "dry" and "do", so it also reads "won't stop till it's done".
5. **GROUNDED = 禁足** (no leaps): keeps the "grounded as a punishment" pun.
6. **FOG BITES = 迷雾反扑** ("the fog strikes back"), not literal.
7. **SECOND WIND = 续命** (also the HUD pod): slangy "extend your life"; 回气 is the neutral option.
8. **CHALICE CONSUMED! = 圣杯已吞下！**: the slime swallows the chalice; check the joke lands.
9. **Ford joke** `(ANY COLOR YOU LIKE)` = （颜色随你挑）: Henry Ford's quote isn't well known in China; kept as a plain
   promise.
10. Story lines: 7 uses Q弹 (bouncy, jelly-like) for "jiggles"; 4 `STILL NOT DRY` = 但也没干 (also reads "didn't do
    it either"). 1 `UP IS THAT WAY` = 上面在那边 (deadpan, slightly odd on purpose).
11. **PAID RUN = 有奖局** and **DESCENT = 深入**: functional choices, Descent is switched off today.
12. **GOO NOSE = 黏液鼻**: the pun is lost (workshop is switched off).
13. `language.title` (LANGUAGE screen, new in code, not yet in messages.pot) is already translated: 语言.
10. **RANDOMIZER = 随机关卡** and **RESUME = 回到游戏 / 回到本局** (fix pass F4): chosen for legibility at 10 px over
    随机模式 / 继续; 关卡 is also "level", so check 随机关卡 reads as the randomizer portal and not one level.
    DESCEND said 继续深入 until R4 (继 is the same dense glyph); now 深入下层, and 继 is used nowhere on a button.
