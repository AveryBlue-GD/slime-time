# Brazilian Portuguese (pt_BR) glossary

Brazilian Portuguese, `você` implied; buttons are infinitives (`REINICIAR`, `COMPRAR`), hints use the
imperative (`ESCOLHA UMA`, `TOQUE EM PULAR`, `APERTE`). Brazilian gamer vocabulary (`SKIN`, `SAVE`,
`TICKET`, `PULAR`, `TELA CHEIA`). ALL CAPS with accents (`Á Â Ã À Ç É Ê Í Ó Ô Õ Ú`). Western digits,
`.` decimals. Plurals (`n > 1`): form 0 = 0 and 1, form 1 = 2 and up. Widths measured with the slimy
font advances (`assets/fonts/pixel.fnt`, 16 px titles x2).

CATACUMBAS SINISTRAS, FORTALEZA CONGELADA, CELA DE OBSIDIANA, CAVERNAS DE CRISTAL, CÂMARA VULCÂNICA.

CRONÔMETRO, CHAMELEON. Upgrades: MAIS SLIME, VELOCIDADE, BRILHO, FORÇA NA PAREDE. Workshop: SEGUNDO
FÔLEGO (HUD pod FÔLEGO), CARGA RÁPIDA, GRUDENTO, ÍMÃ, FARO DE GOSMA. Abilities: RIFLE DE SLIME,
BOMBA DE SLIME, SLIMAKAZE.

## Overflows (translator comments `# overflow:` in pt_BR.po)

- `common.back` VOLTAR 36 px > 32: the standard word; the BACK button needs 4 px more.
- `upgrades.tab_coins` MOEDAS 36 px > 32.
- `common.buy` COMPRAR 42 px > 40 (1 px of ink over).
- `upgrades.tab_tickets` TICKETS 40 px fits the real 44 px tab (same as English).

## Doubts for a native reviewer

- `title.tagline` ESCAPE DA NÉVOA / OU SEQUE TENTANDO: plays on "ou morra tentando".
- `heatmod.grounded.name` DE CASTIGO: pun on a grounded kid (no leaps).
- `result.choice.new_maze` / `replay_maze` use MAPA (LABIRINTO does not fit 76 px).
- `hub.station.random` RANDOMIZADOR (gamer loanword) vs. a Portuguese word.
- `ticket` = TICKET (loanword) rather than BILHETE / FICHA.
- `boon.greedy.name` GOSMA GULOSA (greedy as gluttonous), `workshop.nose` FARO DE GOSMA (GOO NOSE),
  `ability.kaze.desc` INVESTIDA (WALL DASH), `boon.sense.desc_2` A 6 CASAS (tiles).
- Story lines: 1 (PLOFT as the splat sound), 5 (UM SLIME NO MEIO DO SLIME), 7 (SLIME NÃO TREME.
  ELE BALANÇA.).
- `theme.volcanic` CÂMARA VULCÂNICA and `theme.dungeon` CALABOUÇO SOMBRIO lose the English alliteration.
