# Hindi (hi) glossary

Standard everyday game Hindi in Devanagari. Informal `तुम` register; buttons use the `-ओ` imperative
without a pronoun (`खेलो`, `घुमाओ`, `कूदो`). Common gaming loanwords are written in Devanagari
(`लेवल`, `स्किन`, `अपग्रेड`, `हब`, `रन`, `सीड`, `बेस्ट`). Western digits. Full stop `।` only in the
story one-liners; UI labels have no full stop. Plurals: form 0 = 0 and 1, form 1 = 2 and up.
Widths measured with the real chain (slimy font 8 px + Noto Devanagari fixed 14 px / 18 px titles):
no string over its budget.

## Doubts for a native reviewer

- `title.tagline` "ESCAPE THE FOG / OR DRY TRYING" = `कोहरे से निकलो / या कोशिश में सूख जाओ` (echoes "die trying"; is the joke clear?).
- `result.chalice` `प्याला गटक लिया!` (gulped the chalice) for CHALICE CONSUMED.
- Level names: alliteration kept where natural (`काल कोठरी`, `काई की खदानें`, `डूबा देवस्थान`, `काले काँच की कोठरी`, `कब्रों की कंदराएँ`, `क्रिस्टल कंदराएँ`); `रेतीला देवालय` (Sandstone Sanctum) and `ज्वालामुखी तहख़ाना` have none.
- Story lines 2, 4, 7, 8, 10 are free transcreations (e.g. 7 `स्लाइम काँपता नहीं। थिरकता है।`, 8 `तुम्हें कोई नहीं भूलेगा।`).
- `workshop.nose` GOO NOSE = `चिपचिप नाक` (no pun); GLIMMERS = `चमक` (same word as GLOW TRAIL `चमक लकीर`).
- Skins: LIME = `हरा नींबू` vs LEMON = `नींबू`; CRIMSON = `किरमिज़ी` (may be unfamiliar to kids; `गहरा लाल` is the plain option).
- Heat mods: FOG BITES = `कटखना कोहरा`, GROUNDED = `ज़मीन से बँधे`.
- "ANY COLOR YOU LIKE" (Henry Ford joke) = `जो रंग चाहो`; the reference is lost in Hindi.
- `hub.station.quit` `बाहर जाओ` vs the dialog button `छोड़ो`.
- `wheel.won` written as `जीत लिया: {name}!` to avoid gender agreement with the hat name.
- `pot` = `पॉट` everywhere (loanword) only because `गुल्लक` overflowed the 80 px HUD pod.
