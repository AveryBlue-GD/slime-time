# Slime Time polish research

Screens reviewed: hub (unlock-all), levels 1/5/10 (demo scene), title. Level shots have a green goo trail on dark noisy fog.

## A. Diagnosis (what reads amateur)
1. **Hub is one green slab.** Floor, rim, fog and wall all sit in the same mid-green with near-equal value, so there is no focal hierarchy. The floor grid of jelly tiles plus scattered bright speckles is a uniform noisy texture; nothing leads the eye to a station. Stations are tiny (about 20 px) props floating on it with no cast shadows or contact lights, so they feel pasted on. The hub's background is black void with a few stray drips.
2. **Levels: the fog is the biggest offender.** Large blobby cloud noise in a flat mid-saturation colour (olive on L5, brick red on L10, grey on L1) fills 90% of the screen at similar brightness to the walls. Blotchy low-frequency noise at full-screen scale reads as a debug texture, not as darkness. A "pitch-black maze" should have real black; instead there is a muddy mid-tone, so the revealed trail has weak contrast against it.
3. **Theme identity is only a hue swap.** L1, L5 and L10 share an identical layout of fog, trail and walls; only the fog tint changes. Nothing is themed in shape or motion (no embers on L10, no drips on L1, no spores on L5).
4. **Lighting is absent.** The slime emits no light, the chalice has no glow, and walls have no rim light or falloff. The premise (goo trail reveals the map) never feels like light.
5. **UI is mixed-style.** The HUD is small grey-green rounded boxes, thin 1 px frames and tiny 8 px text. The bar frames are inconsistent with the title's chunky 16 px dripping letters. The title screen has an unrelated flat dark maze behind it, a tiny 1 px-bordered button against giant tagline text (scale clash), and two identical slimes just standing.
6. **Palette discipline.** Greens are used for the slime, trail, UI, text, hub and title simultaneously, so nothing is "the" accent. Lacking a complementary accent (warm gold or magenta for goal and interactables), the screen has no pop.
7. **Static.** No visible secondary motion, particles, vignette, screen shake, or squash on screenshots; everything looks like a flat render.

## B. Quick wins (S/M, do first)
1. **Crush the fog to near-black with a dark vignette and a small light radius around the slime.** (Hyper Light Drifter: soft light layered on a limited-palette base, and Hollow Knight's lantern darkness.) Make fog 85 to 95% black, tint only the highlights; add a radial `light` falloff centred on the slime so the trail within about 40 px glows and the farther trail dims. Godot: edit the fog/mist ColorRect shader (`assets/shaders/*fog*`, `mist` in `hub_room.gdshader`) to multiply by a vignette, plus a `PointLight2D` or a single uniform `vec2 slime_px`. No arrays needed. Effort S. Impact: very high. Fixes diagnosis 2 and 4.
2. **Goo glow and bloom on the trail.** (Noita/Dead Cells emissive look.) Draw the trail additively with a 1 px lighter edge ramp and a 2 to 3 px soft halo sprite; optionally a cheap 3-tap blur additive layer at 1/2 res. Godot: `CanvasItemMaterial` blend_mode = ADD on a trail glow node, or WorldEnvironment glow (Compatibility renderer supports glow on desktop but is costly on web, so prefer the manual additive sprite). Files: the trail/reveal script and shader. Effort S/M. Impact: high.
3. **Warm accent colour for the goal and hazards.** (Hyper Light Drifter's magenta/gold accents against cold fields; Don't Starve's selective colour.) Chalice, coins and UI highlights get gold/orange with a pulsing PointLight2D glow plus sparkles; keep everything else cool. Palette edit in `tools/make_placeholder_art.py` PAL and chalice sprite. Effort S. Impact: high.
4. **Ambient theme particles per level.** (Dead Cells lots of particles; Celeste dust/snow.) `CPUParticles2D` (not GPU; lighter on web) per theme: embers (L10), snow (L7), spores (L2/L5), dust (L3), drips (L1/L4), bubbles. 20 to 40 particles, 1 to 2 px, additive. Files: level theme config, a new `scripts/fx/ambient.gd`. Effort S. Impact: high.
5. **Juice on movement and events.** (Vlambeer "Art of Screenshake" talk and Nijman's juice talk; Celeste.) Squash/stretch tween on land/launch/turn, a few trail-drop splat particles, a short camera kick plus decaying shake on bumps and collection, a coin pop with 4 to 6 particles and a 1-frame hit-stop, ease the meter bar flash when low. Tween plus Camera2D offset. Files: slime script, camera, collectibles. Effort S/M. Impact: high on feel.
6. **Outline/shading consistency pass on the slime and props.** (Lospec "pixel art outlines" colored-outline article, SLYNYRD palette ramps.) Use selective dark-hued outline (not black), a light rim on the top-left, and one consistent light direction on all sprites, including the hub stations; add 1 px contact-shadow ellipses under every station and the slime. Files: `tools/make_placeholder_art.py` and the shadow in hub/entity scenes. Effort M. Impact: medium/high.
7. **UI restyle with one frame system.** (Loop Hero, Dome Keeper: chunky 9-slice frames with a consistent bevel, one accent.) Make a single 9-slice panel (2 px dark outline, 1 px highlight, gold corner pips), use it for the HUD pills, buttons and boards; raise the HUD text contrast; make the button the same scale as the title. Files: `scripts/ui/ui_kit.gd`, the theme resource, title scene. Effort M. Impact: medium/high.

## C. Bigger projects
8. **Hub lighting rework.** (Don't Starve's warm pools of light against cool dark.) Darken the whole hub floor 40%, give each station a warm/coloured `PointLight2D` or shader pool matching its identity (cauldron green-orange, portal purple, wardrobe gold), add rim light and a stronger radial vignette, and cut the floor speckle noise. Files: `assets/shaders/hub_room.gdshader`, `scripts/hub.gd`. Effort M/L. Impact: very high.
9. **Per-theme palettes with hue-shifted ramps (4 to 5 colours per ramp).** (SLYNYRD Pixelblog 1 on hue-shifted ramps, Lospec ramp guidance; HLD's regionally distinct palettes.) Define 10 themed ramps: shadows shift toward the ambient colour (cool/purple), lights toward the light colour (warm/yellow), plus a 1-colour accent each. Apply to wall tiles, fog tint and trail. Then do a pixel-diff test that touching colours are distinguishable at 1x. Files: `tools/make_placeholder_art.py` PAL, theme resources. Effort L. Impact: very high.
10. **Parallax depth and themed fog layers.** (Hollow Knight, Dead Cells layered backgrounds.) Replace the single blobby fog with 2 slow-scrolling layers of small-scale banded fog (dithered, 4 steps), plus a far background silhouette layer per theme (stalactites, pillars, crystals). Use `ParallaxBackground` or UV offset in the shader; Bayer-matrix dither as a constant `const` 2x2/4x4 lookup with no runtime-indexed arrays (compute the Bayer value arithmetically). Files: fog shader, level scene. Effort M/L. Impact: high.
11. **Wall and tile autotile detail with ambient occlusion.** (Hollow Knight/Blasphemous heavy material detail.) Add inner-edge shadow tiles, moss/crystal/ice accent decals per theme, 2 to 3 random tile variants, and a 1 px top highlight so walls read as volume. Files: tileset art, `tools/make_placeholder_art.py`, the level builder. Effort L. Impact: high.
12. **Title screen and transitions.** (Celeste/Eastward title polish.) Replace the flat maze with a layered parallax scene: dark vignette, drifting spores, a lit slime silhouette on a glowing chalice, logo bobbing with a goo-drip animation, a light sweep once every few seconds, and a circular iris-wipe or goo-fill transition between hub and levels. Files: `scripts/ui/title.gd`, `scenes/title.tscn`, a new transition shader on a CanvasLayer. Effort M. Impact: medium/high (first impression).

## Cross-cutting guardrails
- Keep 320x180 integer scaling; snap all light and particle positions to whole pixels.
- No run-time-indexed arrays in shaders (Android WebGL): use constants, unrolled loops or arithmetic dither.
- Prefer CPUParticles2D and additive sprites over full-screen blur passes for the web export; cap particles per level.
- Keep colour-grade/vignette as one cheap full-screen pass at 320x180.
