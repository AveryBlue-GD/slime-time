# DRY TRYING: creator and press outreach

Source of truth for outreach. Edit this file in place; it goes to `docs/marketing/outreach.md` in the repo.
Last updated 2026-10-20. Rules applied: only brief.md facts, no HEAT or DESCENT, secret skins only as silhouettes,
no carry-over promise, COMING SOON until there's a store link.

**Before sending anything, check each name:** open the channel or site, confirm they posted in the last 2-3 months
and still cover games like this, and take the business email from their About page or contact page. This list was
built from general knowledge plus web search on 2026-10-20 and was not verified page by page, so some channels may
have changed focus. Never send a full-game key to an address that isn't listed on the creator's own channel.

---

## When to send

| Moment | Who | What to send |
|---|---|---|
| Steam page live (early Nov) | Press tier B, small creators | Short heads-up + Short A + press kit link |
| Web/Steam demo live (Nov-Dec) | All creators | The demo link, one GIF, press kit |
| 1-2 weeks before Next Fest (Feb 8-15, 2027) | Next Fest roundup writers and demo channels | Demo link + "in Next Fest Feb 22 - Mar 1" |
| Launch (date TBD) | Everyone who replied or covered the demo | Full-game key (via Keymailer/Woovit or verified email) |

## Email template (creators and press)

Keep it under 120 words. Change the first line for every person.

**Subject:** DRY TRYING: a slime that lights the maze with its own goo (free demo)

> Hi {name},
>
> {One line on why them: "I saw your video on {game} and think this fits your channel."}
>
> I'm Conner, the developer of **DRY TRYING**. A slime glides through pitch-black, fog-filled mazes. Its goo trail lights the way, but every move drains it, so you race to the golden chalice before you dry up. After the 10 story levels come endless random mazes, hard mode and medals, plus over 1,000 ways to dress your slime.
>
> Free demo: {demo link}
> 25-second clip: {Short A link}
> Press kit (screenshots, GIFs, logo): {press kit link}
>
> The game has no licensed music, so it's safe to stream and monetize. Steam page: {link, or "coming soon"}.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> Conner
> {Discord / social link}

Before launch, offer the demo only. At launch, add: "Happy to send a Steam key for the full game."
