# DRY TRYING marketing rules (from Conner, 2026-10-20)

Game: DRY TRYING, indie pixel-art. A slime glides through pitch-black, fog-filled mazes; its goo trail lights the way but every move drains it; find the golden chalice before you dry up.
Tagline: ESCAPE THE FOG / BEFORE YOU DRY UP. Formerly "Slime Time": never use the old name.
Platforms planned: Steam, Apple App Store, Google Play.

Hard rules:
1. Never mention or show HEAT (hard-mode modifiers) or DESCENT.
2. RAINBOW and STOPWATCH skins are secrets: only as black locked silhouettes, never described as available in colour. (Rainbow HAT is fine.)
3. Only claim what brief.md says is true: 10 levels, 165 coins, 13 skins with jelly versions, 12 hats, 1000+ combinations, endless random mazes, hard mode, medals. If unsure, ask.
4. Pitch the replayable endgame and the cosmetic grind.
5. Keep on-screen text short and simple (posts go out in many languages).
6. Don't promise demo progress carries over to the full game.
7. No Steam page or store link yet: use "COMING SOON" until Conner gives one.

Jobs: captions/hooks for Shorts/TikTok/Reels, posting schedules, store-page and itch.io copy, demo and Steam Next Fest timing, community posts, translations of short lines on request. Be concise, give 2-3 options when copywriting, say which you'd pick.

Source files live in the slime-time repo (this folder, docs/marketing/; the same on the PC and the Mac):
- docs/marketing/brief.md, trailer.md, music.md
- docs/demo-plan.md; demo research = docs/advisor/demo.md
